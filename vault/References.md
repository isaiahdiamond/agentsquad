# References

Mockups made with Higgsfield on Oct 17. They show the look and the screen order, not exact layouts. Text inside the images is often garbled; real UI text is built in code.

Characters in these mocks look like heroes. Ignore that: per [[Decisions]], agents are engineers in normal clothes.

## Style

- Locked style: 32-bit chibi isometric pixel art. Search terms: "chibi isometric pixel art", "isometric RPG sprite".
- Style reference: the "Adventurer Pixel Art" sheet (4 facings on an isometric tile). Saved at `assets/ref/style-reference.png` (add it by hand).

## UF inspiration

- Smathers Library: Collegiate Gothic, opened 1926, center of the campus historic district, Grand Reading Room with a large 1953 mural. Keep any mural generic.
- Campus: red brick, cream stone trim, red tile roofs, a brick clock tower, live oaks with Spanish moss. No logos or mascot.
